# How do you define success for a cybersecurity project?

[Back to questions](../../README.md#cybersecurity-domain)

**Level:** Middle · **Topic:** Cybersecurity Domain · **Source review:** 2026-10-30

## Answer

Translate the business risk into observable outcomes. Use NIST CSF to structure the conversation around Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover, then choose the relevant scope. Deploying a tool is an output; reducing exposure or improving response is an outcome.

For an endpoint rollout, define coverage of relevant assets, policy effectiveness, exception handling, operational ownership, and verification. Agree a baseline and evidence with the security owner. Do not claim that installing a product establishes compliance or eliminates risk. Identify residual exposure and who can accept it after the project closes.
