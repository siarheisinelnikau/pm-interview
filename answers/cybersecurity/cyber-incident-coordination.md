# What is the PM's role during a suspected security incident?

[Back to questions](../../README.md#cybersecurity-domain)

**Level:** Senior · **Topic:** Cybersecurity Domain · **Source review:** 2026-10-30

## Answer

Activate the established incident process and clarify who is incident commander, technical lead, communications owner, and decision authority. Support coordination, timelines, resource access, and business-impact tracking. Follow the security team's evidence-preservation and containment direction.

Bring privacy, legal, and regulatory owners into notification decisions; reporting duties depend on facts and applicable requirements. Avoid improvising a universal notification deadline or publishing unverified attribution. Preserve appropriate records and keep sensitive incident details restricted. Plan recovery validation and post-incident actions. A PM supports the response structure rather than taking forensic or containment decisions away from qualified responders.
