# How do Kanban and WIP limits help a team finish work?

[Back to questions](../../README.md#agile-scrum-and-kanban)

**Level:** Junior · **Topic:** Agile, Scrum, and Kanban

## Answer

Kanban makes workflow, policies, and work in progress visible so the team can manage flow. Agree what started and finished mean, how work is selected, and how blocked items are handled.

If five items are waiting for one reviewer, starting more development increases the queue. A WIP limit encourages helping complete or unblock existing work before starting another item. Treat exceptions explicitly and inspect their effect. A board with unlimited hidden work provides little control. WIP limits should be adapted using flow evidence and team discussion; they are not a quota for measuring individual busyness.
