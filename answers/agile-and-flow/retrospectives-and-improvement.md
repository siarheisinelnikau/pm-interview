# How would you turn a retrospective into measurable improvement?

[Back to questions](../../README.md#agile-scrum-and-kanban)

**Level:** Middle · **Topic:** Agile, Scrum, and Kanban

## Answer

Choose a recurring problem using observations rather than impressions. Explore contributing conditions, agree one or two changes within the team's influence, and name owners and a review date. State what evidence would indicate improvement.

If reviews delay completion, trial a protected daily review window for two weeks and inspect waiting time and escaped defects. The aim is to learn whether the change helps, not to prove the first idea correct. Escalate structural constraints that the team cannot resolve. Repeatedly listing the same issue without allocating authority, time, or follow-up makes a retrospective ineffective.
