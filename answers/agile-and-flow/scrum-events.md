# What decisions should Sprint Planning, Daily Scrum, Review, and Retrospective support?

[Back to questions](../../README.md#agile-scrum-and-kanban)

**Level:** Junior · **Topic:** Agile, Scrum, and Kanban

## Answer

Sprint Planning aligns on the Sprint Goal, selected work, and an approach. The Daily Scrum lets Developers inspect progress toward that goal and adapt their plan. The Sprint Review inspects the outcome with stakeholders and informs what comes next. The Retrospective identifies improvements to effectiveness and quality.

A PM can help ensure external decisions and stakeholder availability do not undermine these events. Keep general status reporting outside the Daily Scrum when it would displace its purpose. A Review should provide useful feedback, and a Retrospective should lead to an improvement that someone can follow through.
