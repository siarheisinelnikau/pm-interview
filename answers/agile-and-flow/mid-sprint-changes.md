# How should a Scrum team respond to urgent work during a Sprint?

[Back to questions](../../README.md#agile-scrum-and-kanban)

**Level:** Middle · **Topic:** Agile, Scrum, and Kanban

## Answer

Clarify urgency, user impact, and whether the work threatens the Sprint Goal. Developers and the Product Owner can negotiate scope as more is learned, while preserving the goal and quality expectations. Only the Product Owner has authority to cancel a Sprint when its goal becomes obsolete.

For a genuine production incident, use the agreed incident process and make the interruption visible. Reforecast affected delivery and review whether repeated interruptions need a different capacity or workflow policy. Do not silently add work while holding the original forecast fixed. Also avoid declaring all stakeholder requests emergencies merely because they arrive from senior people.
