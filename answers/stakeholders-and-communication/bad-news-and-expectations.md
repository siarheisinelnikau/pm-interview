# How do you communicate a missed commitment to a client?

[Back to questions](../../README.md#stakeholders-and-communication)

**Level:** Middle · **Topic:** Stakeholders and Communication

## Answer

Communicate promptly with verified facts, user or business impact, uncertainty, and ownership. Explain the next actions and when a reliable update will be available. Avoid guessing a new date before checking the remaining work and dependencies.

If the launch cannot proceed because reconciliation failed, explain what failed, what exposure it creates, and which recovery options are being assessed. Recommend an option once its trade-offs are understood. Record the decision and update affected commitments through the agreed process. Maintain a predictable update cadence; repeated optimistic promises made without new evidence will damage trust more than a candid forecast.
