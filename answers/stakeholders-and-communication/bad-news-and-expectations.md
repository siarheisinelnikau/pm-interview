# How do you communicate a missed commitment to a client?

[Back to questions](../../README.md#stakeholders-and-communication)

**Level:** Middle · **Topic:** Stakeholders and Communication · **Reviewed:** 2026-10-30

## Answer

Communicate promptly with verified facts, user or business impact, uncertainty, and ownership. Explain the next actions and when a reliable update will be available. Avoid guessing a new date before checking the remaining work and dependencies.

If the launch cannot proceed because reconciliation failed, explain what failed, what exposure it creates, and which recovery options are being assessed. Recommend an option once its trade-offs are understood. Record the decision and update affected commitments through the agreed process. Maintain a predictable update cadence; repeated optimistic promises made without new evidence will damage trust more than a candid forecast.

## Separate facts from the next forecast

A useful first update says: “Reconciliation failed for this transaction set. We have stopped this release, finance and engineering are assessing the affected records, and the next evidence-based update is at 15:00.” Avoid promising resolution by that time unless justified; it is an update commitment.

Once the facts support options, explain the revised forecast, business consequences, recommendation, and decision needed. Distinguish a temporary containment from a complete fix.

Maintain trust by making uncertainty and follow-through visible. Record changes to contractual or executive commitments through the relevant process, and confirm that the same message reaches support and other teams affected by the decision.
