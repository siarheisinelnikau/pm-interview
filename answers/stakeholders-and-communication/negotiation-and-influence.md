# How do you influence a decision when you have no direct authority?

[Back to questions](../../README.md#stakeholders-and-communication)

**Level:** Senior · **Topic:** Stakeholders and Communication

## Answer

Understand the other party's objectives and constraints, then build a shared view of the facts. Offer options that connect the project need to their priorities. Make the request concrete, including effort, deadline, and consequences.

If an infrastructure team cannot provide an environment, investigate queue position, prerequisites, and possible smaller requests. A limited environment or reserved validation window might unblock the critical path. Confirm the agreement in the normal decision record and recognize shared ownership. If negotiation cannot resolve a material conflict, use the agreed escalation path. Repeating “the project is important” does not resolve competing demands.
