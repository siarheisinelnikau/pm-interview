# How would you prioritize risks when a probability-impact score is misleading?

[Back to questions](../../README.md#risk-and-issue-management)

**Level:** Middle · **Topic:** Risk and Issue Management

## Answer

Use a score to structure discussion, then consider urgency, detectability, interdependence, exposure duration, and the severity of plausible outcomes. Check whether the scale hides low-frequency events with unacceptable consequences.

A patient identity mismatch may deserve urgent prevention even when unlikely. A vendor outage might be tolerable if a tested fallback exists. Separate evidence from subjective ratings and document who can accept each exposure. Do not multiply ordinal labels and present the result as precise expected financial loss. Escalate risks beyond agreed tolerance with options and a decision deadline.
