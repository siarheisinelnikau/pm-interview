# How do you scope a proof of concept so it supports a buying decision?

[Back to questions](../../README.md#presales-and-solutions---adjacent)

**Level:** Middle · **Topic:** Presales and Solutions - Adjacent

## Answer

State the uncertain hypothesis, representative scenario, environment, timebox, success evidence, and decision owner. Define what the proof of concept excludes and what additional work production use requires.

A demo may illustrate a workflow; a proof of concept should test a specific uncertainty, such as whether an integration can support the required data. Record security and data constraints before using client information. Close with results, limitations, and next steps. A successful test does not automatically establish scalability, production readiness, or a fixed implementation price.
