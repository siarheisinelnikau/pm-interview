# What should a PM contribute during presales discovery and qualification?

[Back to questions](../../README.md#presales-and-solutions---adjacent)

**Level:** Middle · **Topic:** Presales and Solutions - Adjacent

## Answer

Clarify the client's problem, decision process, budget assumptions, target timing, scope, integration landscape, and delivery responsibilities. Identify unknowns that materially affect feasibility and cost. Sales owns its commercial process; solution and technical owners assess the design.

Recommend bounded discovery or a proof of concept when evidence is missing. Record exclusions, client dependencies, and the basis of any estimate. A valuable presales contribution helps both sides understand what can credibly be delivered and what must be validated before making a promise.
