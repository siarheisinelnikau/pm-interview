# What should a PM contribute during presales discovery and qualification?

[Back to questions](../../README.md#presales-and-solutions---adjacent)

**Level:** Middle · **Topic:** Presales and Solutions - Adjacent · **Reviewed:** 2026-10-30

## Answer

Clarify the client's problem, decision process, budget assumptions, target timing, scope, integration landscape, and delivery responsibilities. Identify unknowns that materially affect feasibility and cost. Sales owns its commercial process; solution and technical owners assess the design.

Recommend bounded discovery or a proof of concept when evidence is missing. Record exclusions, client dependencies, and the basis of any estimate. A valuable presales contribution helps both sides understand what can credibly be delivered and what must be validated before making a promise.

## Surface the estimate-changing unknowns

For a new bank integration, ask who provides access, whether certification is required, what data can be used, and who accepts the result. Record each uncertainty with its effect on price or timing.

A qualified opportunity should have a plausible problem, decision path, delivery boundary, and client participation. If key evidence is unavailable, offer a bounded investigation with an explicit output rather than an unsupported implementation commitment.

Keep commercial negotiation with its owner. The PM's contribution is a credible delivery basis: assumptions, capacity, dependencies, risk, and the conditions under which a forecast remains valid.
