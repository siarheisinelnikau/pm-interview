# How would you handle repeated missed commitments by a team member?

[Back to questions](../../README.md#leadership-and-behavioral-questions)

**Level:** Middle · **Topic:** Leadership and Behavioral Questions

## Answer

Talk privately using specific observations and their impact. Explore unclear expectations, workload, skills, external blockers, or personal circumstances without assuming intent. Agree a practical improvement, support, and review date.

If a specialist misses review windows, check whether several projects have allocated the same time. Adjust commitments or resolve allocation before treating the problem as individual performance. Where expectations remain unmet, work with the person's actual manager and the organization's process. A PM may not have line-management authority. Do not use public comparison, raw ticket counts, or personal speculation as substitutes for constructive feedback.
