# How do HIPAA, GDPR, and patient-data concerns affect the project plan?

[Back to questions](../../README.md#healthcare-domain)

**Level:** Middle · **Topic:** Healthcare Domain · **Source review:** 2026-10-30

## Answer

First establish jurisdiction, data flows, organizational role, and applicable obligations with the privacy owner. HIPAA applies to covered entities and business associates, not automatically to every health app. Where GDPR applies, health information is special-category data; the applicable processing basis and conditions need assessment.

Translate decisions into work: access controls, data minimization, appropriate agreements, retention, logging, incident handling, and safe test data. Removing names alone does not establish de-identification. Plan review of analytics, support tools, and subcontractors as well as the main application. Reassess when data use or suppliers change.
