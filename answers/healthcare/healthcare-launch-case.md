# A hospital wants to launch despite unresolved patient-matching errors. What do you do?

[Back to questions](../../README.md#healthcare-domain)

**Level:** Senior · **Topic:** Healthcare Domain · **Source review:** 2026-10-30

## Answer

Establish the error scenarios, affected workflows, exposure, detection, and consequences with clinical and technical owners. Prevent the affected workflow from being treated as ready while that assessment is incomplete. Bring the issue to the authorized clinical safety and release decision makers with evidence and options.

Options may include correcting and retesting, limiting scope to a demonstrably safe boundary, or postponing. A smaller pilot is useful only if its safeguards address the actual exposure. Plan staff communication, support, monitoring, and fallback workflows. Record the decision and criteria for reconsideration; a calendar target does not resolve patient-identity uncertainty.
