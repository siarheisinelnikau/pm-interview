# What should a PM clarify before planning a healthcare project?

[Back to questions](../../README.md#healthcare-domain)

**Level:** Middle · **Topic:** Healthcare Domain · **Source review:** 2026-10-30

## Answer

Start with the intended users, clinical or administrative workflow, care setting, country, intended use, and consequences of failure. Identify clinical, operational, privacy, security, and regulatory owners. A scheduling tool and a diagnostic function need different assessments.

Map patient identification, staff roles, external records, accessibility needs, and downtime procedures. Ask how the change affects real care delivery, including handoffs outside the software. Plan access to representative clinicians and operational users. Clinical benefit, technical completion, and regulatory readiness are separate questions; establish who can judge each and what evidence they need.
