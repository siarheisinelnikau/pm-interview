# When might healthcare software require a medical-device assessment?

[Back to questions](../../README.md#healthcare-domain)

**Level:** Middle · **Topic:** Healthcare Domain · **Source review:** 2026-10-30

## Answer

Intended use, claims, functionality, market, and risk matter. An administrative appointment function and software that diagnoses or directs treatment may fall under different rules. In the US, FDA guidance explains how it approaches device software functions; other markets need their own assessment.

Engage regulatory and quality specialists before promises about launch date or product claims. Record the assessment and the delivery implications, including controlled requirements, evidence, reviews, and change evaluation where applicable. Do not infer classification from “mobile app,” “AI,” or “healthcare” alone. Revisit the boundary when intended use or claims change.
