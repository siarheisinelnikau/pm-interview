# When would you use analogous, bottom-up, three-point, or relative estimation?

[Back to questions](../../README.md#planning-and-estimation)

**Level:** Middle · **Topic:** Planning and Estimation

## Answer

Use comparable past work for an early estimate, but adjust for differences in scope and context. Bottom-up estimation helps when deliverables are understood. Three-point estimates make optimistic, likely, and pessimistic assumptions discussable. Relative sizing supports team planning where comparisons are more useful than early precision.

For a new payment provider, estimate known interface work and investigate unknown certification steps separately. Include integration, reviews, deployment, and client waiting time when forecasting duration. Do not turn story points into a universal hours conversion. Document the basis and update it as evidence arrives; the technique does not make weak assumptions reliable.
