# What lightweight operating model would you establish for delivery?

[Back to questions](../../README.md#delivery-management---adjacent)

**Level:** Senior · **Topic:** Delivery Management - Adjacent

## Answer

Define intake and prioritization ownership, workflow policies, planning horizons, dependency review, decision thresholds, and operational feedback. Use a small set of shared measures and keep improvement ownership explicit.

Choose meetings and artifacts according to the coordination needed. A weekly dependency conversation and a clear escalation path may help more than an additional status layer. Revisit the model with teams after a trial. The goal is faster useful decisions and completed outcomes; copying another organization's ceremonies does not establish that the model fits yours.
