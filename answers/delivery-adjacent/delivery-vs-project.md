# What changes when moving from Project Manager to Delivery Manager responsibilities?

[Back to questions](../../README.md#delivery-management---adjacent)

**Level:** Middle · **Topic:** Delivery Management - Adjacent · **Reviewed:** 2026-10-30

## Answer

Project management usually centers on a bounded initiative and its constraints. Delivery management often focuses on the continuing ability of teams to turn priorities into working, supported outcomes. Organizations use these titles differently.

Clarify the mandate: cross-team flow, dependencies, impediments, capability improvement, release coordination, and operational feedback may be included. Product priority and technical ownership still need explicit boundaries. Measure whether work reaches users predictably and sustainably, not how many ceremonies the delivery function runs.

## Clarify the continuing mandate

On a temporary migration, a PM may close after accepted transition and remaining obligations are assigned. A Delivery Manager may continue improving how the service teams plan, unblock, integrate, and respond to operational feedback.

Ask whether the role owns one initiative, several teams' delivery capability, or a mix. Agree interfaces with product, engineering, operations, and any PMO.

For an interview, explain a concrete intervention such as reducing review waiting or clarifying intake authority, then show its effect on completion and quality. Broad responsibility for “delivery” should not conceal who actually decides scope, technical risk, or investment.
