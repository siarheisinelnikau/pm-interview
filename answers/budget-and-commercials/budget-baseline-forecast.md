# How do budget, actual cost, committed cost, and forecast-at-completion differ?

[Back to questions](../../README.md#budget-and-commercial-management)

**Level:** Junior · **Topic:** Budget and Commercial Management

## Answer

The approved budget is the authorized funding baseline. Actual cost reflects work or expenses already incurred under the organization's accounting rules. Committed cost represents obligations such as purchase orders. A forecast at completion combines actuals with a current estimate of remaining cost.

If actual cost is 60,000 and remaining work is estimated at 50,000, the forecast is 110,000. Check whether open supplier commitments are already included in that remaining estimate to avoid counting them twice. Reconcile rates, cloud usage, licenses, and support costs. Report forecast variance early; remaining cash alone does not show whether the project can finish.
