# Why should a PM understand margin and utilization without optimizing them blindly?

[Back to questions](../../README.md#budget-and-commercial-management)

**Level:** Middle · **Topic:** Budget and Commercial Management

## Answer

Margin shows the relationship between revenue and relevant costs; specify which costs and margin definition you use. Utilization measures the share of capacity assigned to a defined category, such as billable work. They inform commercial planning but do not measure customer value or delivery health alone.

A highly utilized specialist can become a bottleneck and delay several projects. Unplanned rework can consume margin even when billing looks healthy. Review forecasts, unpaid work, rate assumptions, and service obligations with finance. Favor sustainable flow and good outcomes; maximizing scheduled utilization can increase waiting, overload, and incident risk.
