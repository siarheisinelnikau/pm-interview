# What belongs in operational handover and a time-bounded hypercare period?

[Back to questions](../../README.md#quality-release-and-operations)

**Level:** Middle · **Topic:** Quality, Release, and Operations

## Answer

Transfer service ownership, support contacts, runbooks, dashboards, access, known issues, vendor obligations, and recovery procedures. Agree how incidents are classified and who can make urgent changes. Validate that the receiving team can perform key operations.

Hypercare is a defined period of additional support with staffing, reporting, and exit criteria. For example, exit may require stable critical journeys, no unresolved severe incidents, and an accepted backlog of remaining issues. Avoid ending solely because a date arrived or extending forever because nobody accepted ownership. Leave improvement work with named owners and funded capacity.
