# What belongs in a business case, and when should you revisit it?

[Back to questions](../../README.md#project-management-foundations)

**Level:** Middle · **Topic:** Project Management Foundations

## Answer

Explain the problem, affected users, expected benefits, realistic options, estimated total cost, major uncertainty, and recommendation. Include the option to defer or do nothing. Separate evidence from assumptions and identify who owns each benefit.

Suppose automating claim intake costs 180,000 and is expected to reduce manual handling. Validate transaction volume, actual time saved, adoption, maintenance, and integration costs before presenting a payback claim. Revisit the case when assumptions, regulation, vendor costs, or expected outcomes change. Prior spending alone does not justify continuing; compare the remaining cost and risk with the benefits still achievable.
