# How do projects, programs, portfolios, and ongoing operations differ?

[Back to questions](../../README.md#project-management-foundations)

**Level:** Junior · **Topic:** Project Management Foundations

## Answer

A project has a defined objective and temporary organization. A program coordinates related initiatives to achieve benefits that require joint management. A portfolio allocates investment across initiatives in line with strategy. Operations sustain a continuing service.

For a hospital modernization effort, replacing a scheduling system can be one project; coordinating scheduling, patient identity, and staff adoption can be a program. The investment portfolio may also contain unrelated finance work. Running the scheduling service after transition belongs to operations. Clarify which level you manage: escalating a cross-program resource conflict requires different authority from updating a project plan.
