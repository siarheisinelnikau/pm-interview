# How does a product roadmap differ from a project plan?

[Back to questions](../../README.md#product-management---adjacent)

**Level:** Junior · **Topic:** Product Management - Adjacent

## Answer

A roadmap communicates product direction and intended outcomes over time, with uncertainty appropriate to the horizon. A project plan coordinates scope, work, resources, dependencies, milestones, and control for an initiative.

Connect them without treating every future roadmap item as a dated commitment. For example, a roadmap outcome around onboarding can lead to discovery and several delivery increments. Use evidence to refine scope and forecasts, and explain separately which dates are targets, forecasts, or authorized commitments.
