# How would you connect delivery to product outcomes and experiments?

[Back to questions](../../README.md#product-management---adjacent)

**Level:** Middle · **Topic:** Product Management - Adjacent · **Reviewed:** 2026-10-30

## Answer

Define the user behavior or business result expected to change, establish a baseline, and agree guardrails. Product and analytics owners should select a suitable evaluation method and interpretation, including sample limitations and confounding factors.

For onboarding, pair completion with fraud, support load, and user difficulty. A release can increase completion while creating unacceptable risk elsewhere. The PM plans instrumentation, rollout dependencies, and outcome review ownership. Shipping an experiment is an output; making a supported decision from its evidence is the useful result.

## Specify the evaluation before launch

An illustrative hypothesis is that reducing duplicate form entry will improve eligible onboarding completion without increasing fraud or support burden. Define the population, success measure, guardrails, and observation period with analytics and risk owners.

A before-and-after change alone may reflect traffic mix, seasonality, or another intervention. Use an appropriate comparison or experiment where feasible and interpret uncertainty. Avoid choosing a favorable subgroup only after seeing the result.

The PM ensures instrumentation, consent or privacy requirements, dependencies, and follow-up ownership are planned. The product decision should state what the evidence supports and what remains uncertain.
