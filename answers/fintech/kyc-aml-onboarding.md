# What should a PM include in KYC and AML onboarding scope?

[Back to questions](../../README.md#fintech-domain)

**Level:** Middle · **Topic:** Fintech Domain · **Source review:** 2026-10-30

## Answer

Map the regulated business, jurisdictions, customer types, and risk model with compliance. KYC, customer due diligence, sanctions controls, and ongoing monitoring have related but distinct purposes. FATF recommendations provide an international baseline; local requirements and the institution's policy determine implementation.

Plan identity evidence, beneficial ownership where relevant, screening, review queues, auditability, and ongoing change handling. Include rejected, inconclusive, and manual-review paths in estimates and acceptance. A vendor's pass response is only one input to the institution's process. Review operational capacity and false-positive effects so improved conversion does not simply move unresolved risk into a hidden queue.
