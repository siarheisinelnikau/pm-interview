# How would you handle PSD2/SCA and EU DORA requirements without assuming every fintech has the same obligations?

[Back to questions](../../README.md#fintech-domain)

**Level:** Middle · **Topic:** Fintech Domain · **Source review:** 2026-10-30

## Answer

Establish jurisdiction, licensed entity, product, payment activity, and supplier role. Ask compliance and legal owners which rules, exemptions, technical standards, and contractual obligations apply. Track their decisions as requirements with implementation and evidence owners.

PSD2/SCA concerns payment-services and authentication questions in the applicable context. EU DORA addresses digital operational resilience for entities within its scope, including ICT risk and third-party concerns. Neither is a universal checklist for every financial application worldwide. Recheck the applicable framework when planning a real launch, including legislative transitions. EU DORA and DORA software delivery research share an acronym but have different purposes.
